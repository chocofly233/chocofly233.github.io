This sample post shows how articles, tags, and archives look on the blog.

## What goes here

Questions from reading, lessons from a project, or a detail from an ordinary day can each become a post.

Write new posts in Markdown. Fill in the title, date, and tags at the top, and the post will appear on the homepage and in the archive.
